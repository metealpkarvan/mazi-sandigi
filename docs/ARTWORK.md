# Artwork provenance

Resources/salon.png is an original illustration generated for this project with the built-in image_gen tool using the imagegen skill. The selected output was inspected and copied into the repository. It is not a historical photograph; no UNESCO or ministry photo/video assets were copied.

The bundled source asset is [salon.png](../Resources/salon.png). App screenshots are renderings of the working AppKit views, not generated UI mockups. The app icon is drawn deterministically in scripts/make-icon.swift. Ambient audio is original deterministic synthesis in MaziCore, not a recording or song.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: panoramic interior illustration for the native macOS app Mazi Sandığı, an intimate Turkish nostalgia and personal memory journal. Create one original, refined painterly cut-paper diorama of a modest Turkish family living room around the late 1980s/early 1990s. Landscape composition 3:2. A boxy wooden CRT television with a crocheted lace doily on the right, a small wooden cassette radio at lower left, a low central table with two recognisable slender waist Turkish tea glasses on saucers and a small cologne bottle, upholstered sage sofa behind, a faded red geometric kilim on the floor, lace-curtain window upper left with warm afternoon light, one framed family silhouette on a wall. Keep the objects readable and generously spaced for an interactive memory scene. Warm aged paper, soft ink details, walnut wood, faded terracotta and sage, subtly tactile and sophisticated, inviting and quietly nostalgic. No people in the room, no flags, no mosque, no landmarks, no brand names, no text, no logos, no watermark. An original contemporary illustration, not a claimed historical photograph.

The artwork is distributed with the project's MIT license. A future replacement should be saved and reviewed as a new version, retaining its provenance.
